# Gatecrash terminal surface

- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Audience and job:** An application security tester must turn captured routes
  into an understandable session comparison, often inside a narrow terminal or
  alongside a proxy.
- **Primary task:** See what will be replayed, follow progress, identify which
  lower-privilege sessions received a matching successful response, and open
  the evidence.
- **Proof and content:** Real route, profile, status, similarity, and skip data.
  The built-in doorlab is the only authored demonstration.
- **Direction:** The Midnight Patch Bay. Routes are channels, sessions are
  stable tracks, and state appears as compact labeled lamps. The broken-gate
  mark establishes the access-boundary metaphor without taking over the task.
- **Memorable moment:** The completed access map makes a crossed boundary read
  as one continuous trace from baseline response to wrong session.
- **Constraints:** Inline rendering, no cleared scrollback, useful at 44 columns,
  color-independent meaning, clean JSON stdout, and no persisted secrets.
